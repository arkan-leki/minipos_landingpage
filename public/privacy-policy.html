MiniPOS - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026 · دوایین نوێکردنەوە: ٤ی تشرینی دووەمی ٢٠٢٦

Short version

We collect nothing. Your data stays on your device.

MiniPOS is an offline point-of-sale app for small shops. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising SDKs and no tracking. Sales, products, customers and suppliers are stored only in a local database on your phone.

1. Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, rent or share any personal data with us or with third parties. There is no user account and no cloud service operated by the developer.

2. Where your shop data is stored

All data you enter (products, barcodes, prices, sales, invoices, customers, suppliers, expenses, income, shop profile) is stored only on your device. It leaves the device only when you explicitly choose to:

  • use Save to Google Drive / Share backup in Settings → Backup, or
  • share a receipt or invoice (PDF) through the Android share sheet.

In both cases the Android system file picker / share sheet opens and you decide the destination (Google Drive, SD card, USB, WhatsApp, Telegram, email...). The developer never receives a copy of that file.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Camera - only to scan product barcodes. Photos are not stored or sent.
  • Bluetooth / nearby devices - to connect a thermal receipt printer.
  • Location - only requested on Android 11 and older, because Android requires it to discover Bluetooth printers. The app never reads, stores or transmits your location.
  • Vibrate - short haptic feedback when adding products to the cart.

The app does not request internet access and contains no network code.

4. Third parties

The app contains no third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs. When you use the share sheet you are using Android system components; the privacy practices of the app you send the file to (for example Google Drive) are governed by that provider's own policy.

5. Data deletion

All data is deleted when you uninstall the app or use Settings → Backup → Reset. To keep a copy, create a backup first - the file is then under your control.

6. Children

MiniPOS is a business tool for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL and linked from the app listing.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: arkan.leki@gmail.com
